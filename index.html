About Safe Haven

Safe Haven ADU is an initiative of RNG MANAGEMENT. We aim to connect suitable accessory dwelling units, property owners, community partners, and supportive resources to help women move toward safe, stable housing and greater independence.

Housing with a Purpose

Safe Haven envisions a supportive environment where women can rebuild confidence, strengthen connections, and take steps toward long-term stability.
