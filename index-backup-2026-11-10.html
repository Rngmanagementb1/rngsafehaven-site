About Safe Haven

Safe Haven ADU is an initiative of RNG MANAGEMENT. Our vision is to connect suitable accessory dwelling units (ADUs), community partners, and supportive resources to help women move toward stable housing and greater independence.

How the Initiative Works

The program is designed as a collaborative model between RNG MANAGEMENT, property owners, community organizations, service providers, local agencies, and funders.

  • Identify suitable ADU sites or partner properties
  • Develop or furnish private living spaces
  • Coordinate resident referrals through trusted partners
  • Provide structured support plans for each participant
  • Connect women to income, education, and community resources
  • Measure progress toward independence and stable housing

Housing with a Purpose

More than a roof, Safe Haven envisions a supportive environment where women can rebuild confidence, strengthen connections, and take meaningful steps toward long-term stability.

Who We Serve

The Safe Haven ADU Initiative is designed to support women who need more than temporary shelter—they need a bridge to security, stability, and renewed purpose.

👩🏽

Unhoused Women

Women experiencing homelessness or housing insecurity who are ready for a stable transitional housing environment.

🕊️

Women Rebuilding

Women transitioning from shelters, crisis situations, family disruption, financial hardship, or unsafe living conditions.

👩‍👧

Future Family Expansion

As the model grows, the initiative may expand to include women with children and family-centered support services.

Our Intended Impact

Safe Haven aims to become a replicable community model, beginning with a local pilot and growing through partnerships, funding, and resource connections.
